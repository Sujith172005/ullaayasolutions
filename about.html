About Ullaaya

One Parent Platform For Food Products, B2B Support and Baking Learning

Ullaaya Brothers Solution brings Bumoniis, Osunss and Sarvathaa under one trusted platform. Our work connects customers, learners and food business owners with the right service at the right stage.

Food Products B2B Support Baking Education

Who We Are

Built to make food business simple

We support the complete journey: learning the skill, creating the product, choosing the right packaging, planning quantity, selling to customers and growing with confidence.

Our goal is to keep each brand clear and professional while giving customers one easy way to understand what business we are doing.

Our Main Points

  • Bumoniis focuses on chocolates, baking products, cakes, cookies and desserts.
  • Osunss supports B2B food product planning, bulk supply, packaging and delivery support.
  • Sarvathaa supports recorded courses, offline training, practice area and incubation support.
  • Ullaaya connects all three brands with professional enquiry and guidance.
